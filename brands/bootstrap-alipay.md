## 分析

**著作权:干净。** Bootstrap Icons 全仓 MIT(Copyright (c) 2019-2024 The Bootstrap Authors),
`alipay.svg` 是仓内普通文件,MIT 允许使用/修改/再分发,义务只是保留声明。

**商标:有约束。** 蚂蚁把 "Alipay+" 字标、"+" 符号及相关标识列为注册/未注册商标,
官方页面(Alipay+ Brand Mark Assets)的法律条款写得很明确:

> The "Alipay+" word mark, the "+" symbol and the Alipay+ mark in any stylization or design,
> collectively the "Alipay+" Trade Marks are trade marks (registered or unregistered) of
> Ant Group Co. Ltd. or its affiliate. **Any use of such marks must be authorized in writing by
> Ant Group or subject to an appropriate license** and comply with these Alipay+ Brand Guidelines.

该条款的适用对象写的是**授权合作方**("As an authorized licensee, you acknowledge…"),
并且要求"使用商标的材料须先经蚂蚁审批"(第 3.c 条)。第三方拿它画架构图,不在这份授权的射程内。

**这一条要记清楚:支付宝官方 mark 的下载与使用,官方只给签约合作方。**
